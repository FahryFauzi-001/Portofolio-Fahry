## Problem
Most personal finance apps overwhelm users with cluttered dashboards, opaque cloud syncing, and complex multi-account transfer loops that make logging a daily coffee slower than it should be.

## My Role
Designed and built the entire Android application solo—covering Material 3 UI design, MVVM Clean Architecture, reactive Room SQLite persistence, and schema migrations.

## Solution & Architecture
- **Deterministic Transaction Engine**: Eliminates confusing internal transfer loops by focusing strictly on two clear states (`INCOME` and `EXPENSE`), keeping personal bookkeeping fast and legible.
- **Instant Reactive Aggregation**: Powered by **Kotlin Coroutines** and **Room SQL Reactive Flows** so balances, category breakdowns, and monthly budgets recalculate instantaneously with zero UI lag.
- **Animated Splash & Async Preload**: Preloads the Room database asynchronously behind a lightweight branded splash transition straight into the dashboard.
- **100% Local-First Privacy**: Financial records stay strictly on-device with automated Room schema migrations (`MIGRATION_1_2`, `MIGRATION_2_3`) and optional biometric / PIN security.

## Result
Delivered a crisp, zero-bloat native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose experience that keeps daily financial tracking private, fast, and accessible offline.
