## Problem
Low-cost household and lab fire alarms usually sound only locally—leaving owners completely unaware of gas leaks, smoke buildup, or open flames when nobody is physically inside the room.

## My Role
Wired the embedded sensor circuit, wrote the Arduino C++ monitoring & WiFi alert firmware, and documented the wiring schematic and end-to-end architecture.

## Solution & Architecture
- **Dual-Sensor Hardware Monitoring**: Combines an analog **MQ-2 gas/smoke sensor** (LPG, methane, smoke) with a digital infrared **flame sensor** on an **Arduino UNO R4 WiFi** (5V operation, no separate GSM module required).
- **Immediate Local Feedback**: Drives a green status LED under normal conditions and switches immediately to a red LED + active buzzer when gas exceeds the threshold or flame is detected.
- **Telegram Chat Alert Dispatch**: Sends HTTP alert payloads directly over built-in WiFi to the **Telegram Bot API**, delivering instant fire/smoke warning messages straight to the user's Telegram chat.

## Result
Built and documented a practical, low-cost IoT safety prototype with complete wiring diagrams, reproducible firmware flow, and real-time local + Telegram notification alerts.
