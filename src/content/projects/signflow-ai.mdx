## Problem
Communication barriers between sign language users and non-signers persist in daily interactions because real-time ASL translation requires understanding both spatial hand shapes and temporal motion across video frames.

## My Role
Built the feature extraction pipeline, sequence model architecture (`SignBiGRU`), training/evaluation workflow on unseen signers, and the real-time webcam inference app (`app_webcam.py`).

## Solution & Architecture
- **Spatial Landmark Normalization**: Uses **MediaPipe Holistic** to track 48 spatial keypoints (upper-body pose + left & right hands) plus 2 hand-presence masks—producing a **98-dimensional feature vector per frame** normalized against shoulder center and shoulder width for camera-distance invariance.
- **Temporal Sequence Modeling**: Feeds a **32-frame sliding window** into a 2-layer Bidirectional GRU (`SignBiGRU`, 256 hidden units per direction) with *Temporal Average Pooling* to classify **65 ASL words + 1 `idle` class**.
- **Interactive Real-Time Webcam App**: Live OpenCV inference supporting aspect-ratio toggles (`3:4` / `9:16` / `Raw`), one-key buffer reset, and instant predicted text overlay.

## Result
Evaluated strictly on **unseen signers** in the test split, achieving **75.7% Top-1 accuracy** and **89.1% Top-3 accuracy** across 65 dynamic ASL signs at smooth real-time webcam frame rates.
